Questions

Is Stripe access read-only? Yes. Quillmark asks for read-only access and reads subscriptions, customers and invoices to calculate your monthly numbers. It cannot create charges, issue refunds, edit customers or change a setting, and you can revoke access from your Stripe dashboard at any time.

Who owns my data? You do. Your Stripe figures, bullets, updates and investor list belong to you. We use them only to write and send your updates, we do not sell them or use them to train shared models, and you can export or delete everything when you leave.

Which numbers come from Stripe? Monthly recurring revenue and its change, net new MRR, customer count and revenue churn, each compared with the month before.

What do I write myself? Five rough bullets: what went well, what did not, who joined and what you need. Quillmark turns them into finished paragraphs around your numbers, and you read the result before it goes out.

Why send from my own domain? Investors see your name and your company’s address, not a tool’s mailing domain, and their replies come straight back to you.

How does open tracking work? Each investor receives their own copy, so you can see who opened each update. Some email apps block tracking, so read it as a guide to who to follow up with, not a verdict.

What will it cost? The planned price is $29 per month for everything on this page. We will confirm pricing before anyone is charged.

Who is it for? Founders of subscription startups from pre-seed to Series A, with somewhere between 5 and 100 investors, who bill through Stripe.

What happens after I join? We invite founders in small groups so each one is set up properly. You will get a single email when your invite is ready, and no newsletter.
