FAQ

Before you connect anything.

Is Stripe access read-only?

Yes. Quillmarc will ask for read-only access and read subscriptions, customers and invoices to calculate your monthly numbers. It cannot create charges, issue refunds, edit customers or change a setting, and you can revoke access from your Stripe dashboard at any time.

Who owns my data?

You do. Your Stripe figures, bullets, updates and investor list belong to you. We use them only to write and send your updates. We do not sell them or use them to train shared models, and you can export or delete everything when you leave.

Which numbers come from Stripe?

Monthly recurring revenue and its change, net new MRR, customer count and revenue churn, each compared with the month before.

Does it send from my domain?

Yes, on the one $29 plan. You verify your domain once by adding DNS records, and updates go out from your own address. Replies come straight to your inbox.

How does open tracking work, and how accurate is it?

Each investor gets their own copy, so we can show who opened each update. Treat it as a guide, not proof. Apple Mail Privacy Protection loads emails automatically, which can mark an update as opened when nobody read it, and some email apps block tracking entirely.

How is this different from Visible.vc?

Visible.vc is a broader investor relations platform for founders, with a free plan. Sending from your own domain starts on its $59 a month plan, billed annually. Quillmarc does one job, the monthly update written from your Stripe data, and includes your own domain at $29 a month.

What happens after I sign up?

You get one welcome email from me with your sign-in link. Your 14 days start when you first sign in. Connect Stripe read-only, add your investors and write your first update. We email you about your account and nothing else.

Can I use it today?

Yes. Sign up and you get 14 days with everything on, no card needed. Founders who answer the short questions after signing up help shape what we build next.
