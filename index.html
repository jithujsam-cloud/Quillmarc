How it works

Three steps, once a month.

  1. 01

    Connect Stripe, read-only

    Quillmark reads your subscription data to pull MRR, growth, churn and customer counts for the month. It cannot change anything in your account.

  2. 02

    Type five rough bullets

    Wins, misses, hires, asks. Fragments are fine. You bring the story; the numbers are already filled in.

  3. 03

    Review, send, see who read it

    Quillmark writes the finished update. It goes out from your own domain, and you can see which investors opened it.

Questions

Before you connect anything.

Is Stripe access read-only?

Yes. Quillmark requests read-only access to your Stripe account. It can read subscription, customer and invoice data to calculate your monthly numbers. It cannot create charges, issue refunds, edit customers or change any setting. You can revoke access from your Stripe dashboard at any time.

Who owns my data?

You do. Your Stripe figures, your bullets, your updates and your investor list belong to you. We use them only to write and send your updates. We do not sell your data or use it to train shared models, and you can export or delete everything when you leave.

What will it cost?

The planned price is $29 per month, which includes automatic Stripe numbers, sending from your own domain and per-investor open tracking. We will confirm pricing before anyone is charged.

What happens after I join the waitlist?

We invite founders in small groups so we can help each one set up properly. You will get a single email when your invite is ready. We will not add you to a newsletter.
