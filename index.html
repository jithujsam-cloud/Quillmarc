FAQ

Before you connect anything.

Is Stripe access read-only?

Yes. Quillmark asks for read-only access and reads subscriptions, customers and invoices to calculate your monthly numbers. It cannot create charges, issue refunds, edit customers or change a setting, and you can revoke access from your Stripe dashboard at any time.

Who owns my data?

You do. Your Stripe figures, bullets, updates and investor list belong to you. We use them only to write and send your updates, we do not sell them or use them to train shared models, and you can export or delete everything when you leave.

Which numbers come from Stripe?

Monthly recurring revenue and its change, net new MRR, customer count and revenue churn, each compared with the month before.

Does anything send without me?

No. You read the finished update and change anything you like before it goes out.

How does open tracking work?

Each investor receives their own copy, so you can see who opened each update. Some email apps block tracking, so treat it as a guide to who to follow up with.

What happens after I join?

We invite founders in small groups so each one is set up properly. You get a single email when your invite is ready, and no newsletter.
