Brand guidelines

Quillmark writes a founder’s monthly investor update from live Stripe numbers. Everything about the brand should feel like that letter: calm, exact, well set, and finished.

Version 1.0, October 2026. Quillmark is a working name.

The idea

Founders have written to the people who backed them for as long as there have been backers. The monthly update is a letter, and Quillmark is the pen that already knows the numbers.

So the brand borrows from correspondence and bookkeeping, not from software. Paper grounds instead of white. A serif that reads like a well-printed letter. Figures set like a ledger. One deep green, the colour of a bound account book, used for the thing you should do next.

The test for any decision: would it look at home in a carefully printed letter from a founder to an investor? If it would look at home on a crypto dashboard instead, it is wrong.

Calm
Low density, generous space, one thing per screen. Nothing shouts, blinks or competes.
Precise
Real numbers that add up, exact words, tabular figures. Never a vague claim or a rounded boast.
Premium
Restraint and finish: good type, true colour, careful spacing. No ornaments standing in for quality.
Editorial
Set like a publication. Headlines are sentences, sections are chapters, pictures are chosen, not decorative.

The mark

A ring with a short diagonal tail that crosses the stroke, ending in a small filled square. Read it as a Q whose tail ends in a full stop: the update, written and finished. It sits beside the wordmark, set in Newsreader Medium.

The mark and wordmark are drawn files. Never retype the wordmark in a font; use the supplied SVGs, where the letters are outlined.

Clear space

Keep empty space around the mark equal to the ring’s radius (9 of 32 units) on every side. Around a lockup, the same distance measured from the mark.

Minimum size

Mark alone: 16px. Horizontal lockup: 96px wide. Stacked lockup: 72px wide. Below that, use the mark alone.

Colour

Ink on paper, reverse on green or dark, or brand green on paper. Never brass, never a gradient, never two colours at once.

Do not

Colour

Paper and ink carry the page. Brand green marks the action and fills the occasional whole section. Brass is a jeweller’s amount: a small square, a dot, a rule beside a list. Proportions, roughly: ground 80%, ink 15%, green under 5%, brass a trace.

Display and letters. Headlines, the wordmark, statements and the body of sample updates. Regular 400 for headlines, Medium 500 for the wordmark. Tracking −0.02 to −0.025em at display size, leading 1.0 to 1.05.

Hanken Grotesk

Interface and running text. Body at 18px with leading 1.55, labels at 14px. Regular 400, Medium 500 for labels and buttons, Semibold 600 for run-in questions. Sentence case always.

Geist Mono

Figures only: prices, metrics, dates and times in data. Never for labels, captions or anything a sentence would say. It is how Quillmark sets a ledger.

Voice

Write like a founder who respects the reader’s time: plain, specific, unhurried.

  • Say the specific thing. “Pulls MRR, churn and customer count from Stripe” beats “powerful insights”.
  • Use real numbers or none. No invented customers, quotes, logos, ratings or statistics. Sample figures are labelled as fictional.
  • Short sentences, full stops. No em dashes anywhere. Use a full stop or a comma.
  • Sentence case. For headings, buttons and labels. No all-caps lines, no small tracked labels.
  • Buttons name the action. “Join the waitlist”, the same words everywhere.
  • Calm, not cute. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no puns about quills.

Imagery

Two kinds of picture, and nothing else.

Old pictures of people writing and keeping accounts. Public-domain paintings, ledgers and letters from museum collections. They carry the idea: this is an old and serious ritual. Always credited by title, maker and date. Never presented as customers or the team.

The product, built for real. Fragments of the actual interface with numbers that add up, labelled as sample data. No device frames, no grey bars, no stock photos of people at laptops, no illustrations of rockets or graphs.

Pictures are left untouched: no colour wash, no duotone, no brand tint. Corners take the 16px radius.

Layout and motion

Frame

A 12-column grid with equal outer margins from 16 to 32px. Sections sit 96 to 144px apart. Surfaces are told apart by a tone step, never by a shadow or a divider line.

Radius

6px for controls, 10px for panels inside the product, 16px for sheets, stages and pictures. Nothing else.

Motion

Strong ease-outs that settle like a spring without overshooting: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1). Hover 220ms, small moves 380ms, entrances 820ms staggered 75ms, pictures crossfade over 1.6s. No bounce, no parallax, and everything still works with reduced motion.
